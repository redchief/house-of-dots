# House of Dots: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

House of Dots is a game that runs inside Reddit through Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit). This policy explains what the game stores about you and why.

## What we store

When you play, the game stores:

- **Your Reddit username and user ID.** These identify your seat in a game and are shown to the other players.
- **Your Reddit avatar's image link.** This is used to stamp the boxes you capture. We store the link, not a copy of the image.
- **Game data:** which room you're in, the lines and boxes on the board, scores, and room codes.
- **Leaderboard scores:** your number of wins and boxes captured in each community, listed under your username.

## How long we keep it

- **Game and room data** is deleted automatically 24 hours after the last activity in a game.
- **Leaderboard scores** are kept for as long as the app is installed in a community.

## Invites

If a player invites you by username, the app sends you one Reddit private message containing a room code and a link to the game. The app does not store your username for this, and each room can send at most 5 invites.

## What we don't do

- We don't collect your email, location, or any data from outside Reddit.
- We don't sell or share your data with anyone.
- We don't show ads or use tracking or analytics beyond what Reddit itself provides to app developers.

All data is stored using Reddit's Developer Platform. Reddit's own [Privacy Policy](https://www.reddit.com/policies/privacy-policy) also applies to your use of Reddit.

## Removing your data

To have your leaderboard entries removed, send a message to the moderators of r/house_of_dots. We'll delete them within 30 days.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
